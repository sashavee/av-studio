When you visit the site

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). To deliver pages and keep the site secure, Vercel processes technical data in its server logs: IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. We do not use this data to identify you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working and secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Vercel may process data in the USA under safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

  • Language choice. If you pick a language with the LT / EN / RU buttons, your browser remembers it in local storage (“lang”). It stays on your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

When you contact us

When you press “Send request” in the form on the main page, your answers (what you need, name, phone, and, if you add them, your business and website) are passed through our hosting provider Vercel and arrive as an email in our mailbox. Nothing is sent before you press the button.

We use these details, and any message you write to us yourself, only to contact you and prepare an offer (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), steps before a contract). If we don't start working together, we delete the request and the conversation within 12 months. If you write to us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp (Meta) handles the messages under its own privacy policy. Emails to hello@avstudio.agency are stored by our email provider, Zoho Corporation, in its EU data centre.

Links to other sites

Our portfolio links to websites we built. They have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. Just write to hello@avstudio.agency. You can also complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI, vdai.lrv.lt).

Changes

If the site or the services we use change, we will update this page and the date above.
