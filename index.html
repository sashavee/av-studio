FAQ

How much does a website cost?

From €200 for a landing page. We give you the exact price after a short chat, and it stays fixed.

How fast will it be ready?

A landing page takes up to a week. A bigger site takes a week or more; we give you the exact date after the brief.

What do I need to prepare?

Almost nothing: answer five short questions and send photos if you have them. We'll help with the texts.

What if I don't like the design?

First you get three design drafts to choose from, and we adjust the one you pick. Two rounds of edits are included.

Are there monthly fees?

Usually no. Hosting is free for most sites, so you only pay for the domain, about €10–20 a year. Support is optional, €25 a month.

What if I need a shop, online booking or an admin panel?

We can build it. Such sites need paid hosting, so we price the development and the hosting separately and tell you both before we start.

I have no texts or photos. What now?

We'll write the texts with you, that's included. Photo and video shoots cost extra if you need them, or we pick suitable free stock photos.

Can you add analytics?

Yes, on request. We usually set up cookie-free statistics, so no consent banner is needed. Google Analytics works too; then we add a consent banner.

Can I pay in parts?

Yes: part before we start, the rest after launch.

I know nothing about tech.

You don't have to. Domain, hosting, email, Google profile: we set it all up.
